Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Alfajiri Press respects your privacy. The CST Exam Study Guide 2026–2027 app is designed to work without an account or sign-in.

Information We Collect

The app does not directly collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Study progress, test results, bookmarks, flashcard activity, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device.

In-App Purchases

Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. Alfajiri Press does not receive or store the user’s full payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s privacy policies.

Offline Use

The app’s study content and progress features are designed to operate locally on the device. No account is required to access the free preview or purchased content.

Notifications

If notification features are enabled in a future version, users will be asked for permission through the device’s operating system. Users can control notification permissions through their device settings.

Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for individuals preparing for professional certification examinations and is not directed toward children under 13.

Independent Study Resource

The app is independently produced by Alfajiri Press. It is not produced, approved, sponsored, or endorsed by any certifying organization and contains no live examination questions.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
