For assistance with purchases, purchase restoration, app navigation, or study features, email johnthuko@gmail.com.

When requesting assistance

  • Include the app name.
  • Include your device model and iOS version.
  • Briefly describe the problem.
  • Attach a screenshot when appropriate.

Restore a Purchase

  1. Open the app and go to the Dashboard.
  2. Select Upgrade.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.
  4. Use the same Apple ID that originally purchased CST Full Access.

Navigation

Feature screens include a Dashboard back button or an Exit control. Use these controls to return safely to the main Dashboard.

Response Time

Support requests are normally answered within two business days.
